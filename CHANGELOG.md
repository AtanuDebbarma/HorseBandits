# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Places Mounted Pillagers and Mounted Ransackers into existing bandit parties in Empire, Vlandia, and Sturgia. No new hideouts, clans, or map changes. About 35 percent of eligible parties are picked once, and 35 percent of a picked party's regulars are replaced. The rest of the party stays vanilla.
- Mod Configuration Menu page. Each bandit clan has its own switch and a replaced-troops slider from 1 to 80. Looters, Deserters, Mountain Bandits, Forest Bandits, and Desert Bandits start on at 35. Sea Raiders and Steppe Bandits start off.
- War Sails is supported and is not required. Its switch starts on and lists Northern Sea Raiders and Corsairs, both off. Parties at sea, and parties that cannot travel on land, are never changed. Without War Sails those clans do not exist, so the switches do nothing.
- Horse Bandit Chief and Boss can appear in boss parties only, one switch for both, at 50 percent of the replaced troops. Soldier upgrades are not spawned.
- Highwayman can upgrade to Vlandian Brigand Champion, then Vlandian Brigand Banner Knight. The switch starts on. Off restores the base game upgrade, Vlandian Champion.
- Mounted Pillager upgrades to Mounted Huntsman, then Mounted Packmaster. Mounted Ransacker upgrades to Imperial Bandit Horseman, then Imperial Elite Bandit Horseman. Those upgrades need Veteran's Respect.
- Bandit Javelins drop and sell as themselves. Large Bag of Simple Javelins and Stacked Piercing Arrows convert to vanilla items when looted.
- Adding the mod to an existing campaign is supported. Removing it after its troops or items are in the save is not.
- Translation support. All of the mod's text is in `ModuleData/Languages/EN/hb_strings_en.xml`. [TRANSLATORS.md](TRANSLATORS.md) explains how to publish a translation as a separate mod.
